How to play
  • There is a secret line of colors. Your job is to find it.
  • Click a color from the palette, then click a spot to put it there.
  • Click a spot again to make it empty.
  • The same color can appear more than once in the code.
  • After every guess you get marks:
    🟢 right color in the right spot
    🟡 right color but in a different spot
    ⚪ this color is not in the code
  • The marks do not tell you which spot they belong to — that is the puzzle!
